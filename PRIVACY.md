# Privacy

Constellation is local-first. Real vault data, generated indexes, caches, logs, browser state, provider credentials, and private benchmarks are not part of the public distribution.

The public demo corpus is fictional and uses reserved `example.test` domains. External provider use is disabled unless explicitly configured.

Professional hypotheses are limited to observable professional conduct, public mandate, incentives, decision tempo, and collaboration posture. They remain evidence-backed working hypotheses; Constellation does not support mental-health diagnosis, protected-trait inference, hidden vulnerability scoring, or manipulation advice.
