# Constellation

**A private, local-first workspace where your meetings, contacts, decks, and decisions become a searchable intelligence system — not a pile of forgotten files.**

You already have a second brain. Notes apps, CRMs, kanban boards, file folders. The problem isn't capture — it's retrieval. You met someone at a conference six months ago. What did you discuss? What did you decide? Where is their card? Was there a follow-up?

Constellation turns every interaction into a traceable record. Drop a business card and it preserves the original, extracts the text, creates a contact with verified identity, stages a follow-up task, and links everything bidirectionally. Run a LinkedIn profile and it builds a full dossier — experience, mutual connections, recent posts — and files it alongside the card. Record a meeting and the transcript becomes an interaction record with decisions extracted, action items flagged, and participants linked. Every fact in your vault becomes a machine-readable claim with source anchors, so six months later you can ask "what do we know about this market?" and get sourced answers, not vague memories.

Everything runs locally. No cloud, no subscriptions, no data leaving your machine unless you choose to wire in web research — and even then, confidential material stays blocked. Markdown files are the source of truth, so you can read and edit them in Obsidian or any text editor. The SQLite search index is disposable and rebuildable from those files. The web research ladder (SearXNG → Firecrawl → Scrapling → Crawl4AI) stays within your own infrastructure. This is a tool for people who need their strategic memory to be private, durable, and actually useful under pressure — not another SaaS dashboard they'll abandon in three months.
